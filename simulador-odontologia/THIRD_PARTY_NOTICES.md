## Open radiographic cases

The simulator may display radiographic images only when their reuse conditions are recorded. Current policy distinguishes copyright permission from privacy/consent documentation.

- Wikimedia Commons cases: attribution and license are shown with each case. Some are restricted to preview until privacy documentation meets the project’s stricter production threshold.
- Educational CBCT slices created by this project contain no patient data and are approved for production use.
- A desidentified CC BY 4.0 CBCT dataset is recorded as the preferred source for future clinical cases; clinical files are not promoted to production until license, anonymization and provenance are verified for the selected case.

## Universidad Nacional del Oeste — Programa Analítico

The simulator’s curricular map references the publicly published Programa Analítico de Anatomía Normal for Odontología. The program and bibliography are used as academic alignment sources; copyrighted textbook content is not reproduced. Original explanatory text and diagrams are created for this simulator.

## Educational disclaimer

The simulator is designed for education. Schematic nerves, vessels, muscles, articular disc, dental proxies and educational CBCT slices are not patient-specific segmentations, do not encode all anatomical variation and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment planning or other clinical decisions.
